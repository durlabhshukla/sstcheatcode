1789 MILESTONE 1 / 9
The French Revolution
⚡ Cause / Background Autocratic Bourbon monarchy and feudal estate privileges.
📌 Core Event & Action Sovereignty transferred from monarchy to French citizens; ideas of la patrie and le citoyen created a collective identity.
🎯 CBSE Exam Takeaway Marks the first clear expression of nationalism in Europe.
